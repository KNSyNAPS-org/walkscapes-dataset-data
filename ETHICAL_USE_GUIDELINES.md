# WalkScapes — Ethical Use Guidelines
 
The authors request that WalkScapes not be used to:
 
- identify or re-identify individuals;
- track specific persons across locations or datasets;
- infer sensitive personal attributes;
- conduct discriminatory profiling;
- support unlawful or disproportionate surveillance;
- make automated decisions that may adversely affect an individual
  without appropriate human review;
- generate deceptive, defamatory, or malicious content (e.g., deepfakes or fabricated surveillance footage);
- develop military applications, weapons systems, or technologies intended to cause physical harm.
 
Users should conduct an ethical and legal assessment appropriate to their
jurisdiction and intended application.
 
These guidelines express the authors’ expectations concerning responsible
use. They do not modify the CC BY-NC 4.0 license.
